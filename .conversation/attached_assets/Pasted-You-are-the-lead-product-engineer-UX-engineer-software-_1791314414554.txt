==================================================
PRODUCT DEFINITION
==================================================

LOCALBITE is NOT a food-delivery clone.

It is a local-food discovery and cultural guidance platform for first-time visitors.

The core journey is:

DISCOVER
→ UNDERSTAND
→ COMPARE
→ FIND
→ EXPERIENCE

The core problem is:

A visitor may see a local food name but not know:

- what it is
- what it tastes like
- how spicy it is
- what ingredients it contains
- whether it fits their diet
- how much it typically costs
- why prices vary
- where to find it
- how to order it
- how to eat it
- whether it is locally authentic or tourist-oriented
- what cultural significance it has

LOCALBITE solves this problem.

==================================================
PRICE TRANSPARENCY
==================================================

This is a major differentiating feature.

Never present uncertain prices as universally exact.

Prefer:

"Typical price"
"₹120–₹180"

instead of:

"Price: ₹150"

Explain legitimate variation such as:

- local eatery
- restaurant
- tourist area
- premium restaurant
- location
- serving size

Where information is uncertain, clearly label it as an estimate or unverified information.

The UI must build trust rather than create false precision.
